Zhaonan Li

I am a third-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at ASU SCAI, advised by Prof. Ben Zhou.

My research focuses on post-training of large language and vision-language models, including reinforcement learning and distillation for reasoning, compute-efficient training and inference, and robust multimodal reasoning. I also study which tokens decide a model's answer, as a lens on reasoning failures and a signal for efficient post-training.

Previously, I was an applied research intern at Capital One and a research intern at the National Renewable Energy Laboratory (NREL). I received my B.S. (summa cum laude) in Computer Science and Applied Mathematics from Brandeis University and my M.S. in Computer Science from Columbia University.

I am looking for research internships for Summer 2027.
